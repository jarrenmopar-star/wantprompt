Best AI Prompts for Small Business

A small team with good prompts can out-produce a much bigger one. These are the highest-leverage uses.

Big companies have marketing departments, copywriters, and analysts. A small business has you — and AI. The difference between "AI is a toy" and "AI is my unfair advantage" is almost entirely which prompts you use. Here are the five categories that pay off fastest.

1. Marketing copy

Product descriptions, ad angles, landing pages, and social captions are the most common small-business bottleneck. A good prompt turns a rough idea ("we sell handmade soap") into finished copy in minutes. The trick: tell the AI who the buyer is and what makes your product different — generic inputs produce generic ads.

2. Customer email

Complaints, quotes, follow-ups, and cold outreach all get faster with a prompt that sets the tone and the goal. One well-written prompt can handle the email you used to dread: the unhappy customer. It keeps the reply calm, specific, and professional while you stay in control of the final send.

3. SOPs and operations

Every task you do twice should be written down. Prompts that turn a rough process ("here's how we onboard a client") into a clean step-by-step SOP save you from re-explaining it forever — and make your business sellable one day, because the knowledge isn't trapped in your head.

4. Planning and decisions

Pricing, hiring, partnerships, and "should I do this at all" decisions all benefit from a structured prompt: lay out the options, the constraints, and ask for the trade-offs. AI won't make the decision for you, but it will surface the second-order effects you're too close to see.

5. Negotiation and sales prep

Walking into a negotiation with a prompt-generated prep sheet — their likely objections, your walk-away point, your concessions in order — is the closest thing to a cheat code in business. Five minutes of prep changes the outcome of the meeting.

Start with one

Don't overhaul everything at once. Pick the task you did most recently that felt like a chore, find the matching prompt, and run it. That's the whole adoption plan.
