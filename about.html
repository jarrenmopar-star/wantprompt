About WantPrompt

WantPrompt is a free library of 100 hand-crafted AI prompts for writing, marketing, business, coding, image generation, productivity, learning, and fun.

Every prompt is written to be used, not just read. Each one has fill-in-the-blank fields you customize, a copy button, and a built-in free AI that runs it right in your browser — no signup, no API key. Agent Mode takes it further: it drafts, critiques, and polishes your prompt's output in three steps.

Why it exists

Most people get mediocre results from AI because they start from a blank box. A good prompt does the structuring for you — the role, the format, the constraints — so the AI's first answer is already close to what you wanted. WantPrompt exists to hand you that starting point.

Guides

The site also publishes short, practical guides on getting more out of AI: how to write better prompts, prompts for small business, prompts for marketing, prompts for teachers, and prompts for creators.
