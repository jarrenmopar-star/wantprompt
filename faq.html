Frequently Asked Questions

Is WantPrompt free?

Yes. All 100 prompts are free, and the built-in AI that runs them is free too. No signup, no payment, no trial.

Do I need an account or an API key?

No. You open the site, pick a prompt, fill in the blanks, and run it. There is no account system and nothing to install.

What is Agent Mode?

Agent Mode runs a prompt in three steps: it drafts an answer, critiques that draft, then polishes it into a final version. It is for prompts where the first answer is rarely good enough.

Can I use the prompts commercially?

Yes. Use the prompts for your business, your clients, or your content. The prompts are yours to use however you like.

Why is the built-in AI sometimes slow?

The free AI is a shared public service, so it can be slow or briefly rate-limited at busy times. The site retries automatically; if it still fails, copy the prompt and run it in ChatGPT, Claude, or any other AI chat tool.

Can I suggest a prompt?

Yes — email hello@wantprompt.com with the prompt idea and what you would use it for.
