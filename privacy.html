Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

WantPrompt is a free prompt library. This policy explains in plain language what data is involved when you use it. Short version: the site itself collects almost nothing.

What we collect

  • No accounts, no sign-ups. You never enter a name, email, or password to use the site.
  • Favorites stay in your browser. When you tap the heart on a prompt, the saved list is stored in your own browser's local storage. It is never sent to us.
  • No analytics or tracking by this site. We do not run our own analytics, fingerprinting, or tracking scripts.

Try with AI

When you run a prompt with the built-in AI, the prompt text is sent to Pollinations, a third-party AI service, so it can generate a response. Pollinations processes the request under its own privacy policy. Don't paste passwords, private data, or anything sensitive into the AI feature — treat it like any public AI chatbot.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled on this site, Google AdSense may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Google ad settings. See Google's advertising policies for details.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate networks may use cookies to track the referral. Any affiliate relationship is disclosed where the link appears.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version.
