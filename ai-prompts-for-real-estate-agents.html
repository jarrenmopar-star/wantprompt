AI Prompts for Real Estate Agents

Listings, follow-ups, and social posts — the writing load every agent carries, handled in minutes.

An agent's day is half conversations, half writing about those conversations. The prompts below cover the writing half: listings that sell the home, follow-ups that don't sound like templates, and social content that actually gets seen.

1. Listing descriptions

Feed the AI the facts — beds, baths, square footage, the one feature buyers will remember, the neighborhood's one best selling point — and ask for a description under 150 words that leads with the feature, not the floor plan. Then check every fact. AI loves to invent a "newly renovated kitchen" that doesn't exist, and a wrong listing fact is a liability, not a typo.

2. Follow-up messages

The best follow-up prompt includes what you actually talked about: the buyer's budget, the neighborhood they liked, the objection they raised. A follow-up that references the real conversation gets replies; a generic "just checking in" gets archived.

3. Social posts from one showing

One open house becomes a week of content: a just-listed post, a feature spotlight, a neighborhood note, and a "what $X buys here" post. A repurposing prompt that takes one listing and outputs all four formats is the highest-leverage prompt most agents never write.

4. Objection handling

"We want to wait for rates to drop." "Your commission is too high." Prompt for three honest responses to the objection you hear most — not scripts to memorize, but angles to think with. The agent who has thought through an objection answers it calmly; the one who hasn't gets defensive.

The rule for all of it

AI drafts, you verify — especially facts about a property, a price, or a neighborhood. Your license is on every word that goes out under your name.
